Support

Last updated: November 19, 2026
In short: the fastest way to reach the organizing team is the "BNC Support" chat built into the app itself.
In the app
  1. Open the BNC 2026 app.
  2. Tap the BNC Support icon in the header, or the "BNC Support" card on the Home screen.
  3. The first time, enter your email address to start a private conversation -- no password or account needed.
  4. Send your question. An organizer will reply in the same conversation; reopening the app returns you straight to it.
Common questions
The app isn't loading schedule/speaker info
Check your internet connection and pull down to refresh. If it persists, use BNC Support in the app to report it.
I'm not receiving notifications
Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your device's system settings, and that you have an internet connection.
I have a question about the conference itself (registration, venue, schedule changes)
Use BNC Support in the app, or the contact details on the official conference website.
